Delete your account and data

Taxi of Mum & Dad

You can delete your taxi firm and everything in it at any time, from the app or from any web browser. You don't need to reinstall anything.

Delete everything (your whole family)

  1. Open the app, or go to taxi-mum-dad-eu.onrender.com on the phone you use for it.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings.
  3. Scroll to 🔒 Your data and tap Delete our family and all its data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

This deletes straight away: the family, both parents' driver profiles, every child, trip, receipt, rating, promise and job. It can't be undone. If you'd like a copy first, tap ⬇️ Download our data in the same place.

Delete one child

In ⚙️ Settings → Passengers, tap Edit next to the child, then Delete. Their trips, receipts, ratings and promises are removed with them.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. We keep anonymous counts only (for example "a family signed up in November"). Families with no activity for 12 months are deleted automatically. See the privacy notice for details.
