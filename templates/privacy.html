Privacy notice

Last updated {{ policy_date }}

Taxi of Mum & Dad is a fun family app. Parents log lifts, send cheeky receipts, and kids pay them off with jobs. We built it to collect as little as possible. This page explains what we keep, why, and how you control it.

👋 For kids: the short version

  • We only know your first name, the trips Mum or Dad logged, and anything you write, rate or sign on a receipt.
  • We never keep where you went on a map, show you ads, or share your stuff with anyone outside your family.
  • Your receipt link is secret. Only people you or your parents send it to can open it.
  • In Car Bingo you can shout what you spot. Your phone turns your voice into words. We only see which thing you spotted, and we never keep a recording.
  • Your parents can delete everything about you at any time. If you want something deleted, ask them, or ask us.

What we never collect or do

  • No GPS history. Your phone uses location during a trip only to measure distance. Positions are never sent to us or stored.
  • No email addresses, passwords, payment details or photos.
  • No advertising, no selling or renting data, no profiling of children. Sponsors never receive any personal data.
  • No third-party trackers, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other companies.

Car Bingo and voice

Car Bingo is an optional game that can listen for players shouting what they spot. When voice spotting is on, the phone's own speech service turns speech into text. On Chrome that is Google's service, and on iPhone it is Apple's, so audio is processed by them under their privacy terms. The text is matched on the phone itself, and only the result ("Jack spotted a red door, 2 points") is sent to us. We never receive or store audio or transcripts. "Teach my name" saves how the phone hears a name on that phone only. You can turn voice off at any time and play by tapping.

County Collector works the same way: players shout a county they spot on a number plate, and only "Jack spotted Kerry" reaches us. We never record or store anyone's number plate, only the county.

On phones that support it, the games use a "radio-friendly" mode that keeps the phone's microphone open for as long as a game is open, so the car radio doesn't keep cutting out. The same rules apply: speech goes only to the phone's speech service, and nothing is recorded or sent to us. Close the game and the microphone is released. During a phone call the game lets go of the microphone and waits.

Our legal basis

  • To provide the app you asked for: performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Children's details entered by parents, and kids' input on receipts: our legitimate interest in providing a family service (Art. 6(1)(f)). We've weighed this carefully in favour of the child: minimal data, no marketing, short retention and parental control.
  • Anonymous usage counts and security logs: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The app is for parents. Children don't sign up, and they use only the receipt link a parent sends them.

Who helps us run it

The app and its database are hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) by Render and Neon. They act only on our instructions under data-processing agreements. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for a short period for security. If you send a receipt with WhatsApp, that message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. We only prepare the text.

How long we keep it

  • For as long as your family uses the app.
  • Promise signatures are deleted as soon as the promise is closed.
  • Receipt links stop working after 6 months.
  • Families with no activity for 12 months are deleted automatically.
  • When you press "Delete our family", everything is deleted straight away, apart from anonymous counts that can't identify you.

Your rights

In Settings → Your data you can download everything we hold (access and portability), edit any name or job (correction), delete a child or your whole family (erasure). You can also object to any processing or ask us anything at the contact above. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Receipt and invite links use long random codes. Only the drivers in your family can see your family's data. If something ever went wrong, we would tell the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and tell you if you were affected.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app first.
